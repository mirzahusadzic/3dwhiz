Easy explanation of the sample median

As an example, we will calculate the median of the following population of numbers: 1, 5, 2, 8, 7.
Start by sorting the numbers: 1, 2, 5, 7, 8.
In this case, 5 is the median, because when the numbers are sorted, it is the middle number.
If there is an even amount of numbers, the median is the arithmetic mean of the two middle numbers.
As an example of this scenario, we will calculate the median of the following population of numbers: 1, 5, 2, 10, 8, 7.
Again, start by sorting the numbers: 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 10.
In this case, 6 is the median, because when the numbers are sorted, 5 and 7 are the two middle numbers. 
The arithmetic mean of 5 and 7 is 6 (5 + 7 = 12, and 12/2 = 6).



Approximating center of a cluster 'Cc' of the 3D objects.
----------------------------------------------------------

- The center of a cluster is defined as most dense point (with highest concentration of objects) in the 3D space. Depending 
on the location, the most closer object is choosen as optimal seed from which n-tree is built.

- Let 'On' is the count of total objects in the scene. The object 'O' is defined as 3D mesh with finite set of vertices (preferable low-poly meshes).

1. For each 'O(i)' calculate center (3D point) of an bounding volume. AABB bounding volume is calculated by finding the objects minimal and 
   maximal (pMIN and pMAX points) x, y and z values. The center 'Oc' is defined as (pMAX - pMIN) / 2.0.

2. Sort objects center 'Oc' points, by treating each cardianl ax separately. This lead to constuction of separated X,Y and Z arrays.

3. Find arrays medians mX, mY and mZ (median will exclude lonely-marginally objects).

- The appoximated center of a cluster 'Cc' is constructed as 3D point {mX, mY, mZ}.


Selecting the clusters optimal seed object depending on the 'Cc' location
--------------------------------------------------------------------------


- The clusters optimal seed object is considered to be a closest object to the center of a cluster 'Cc'.

1. For each object 'O(i)' calculate the distance 'Od' defined as |Od| = Cc - Oc. This lead to the construction of an array of real numbers 
   'D', which represents distance from the objects center to the center of a cluster.

- The object with minimal distance 'Od' (perfectly 0.0) is choosen as optimal seed object, from wich the n-tree is built.
